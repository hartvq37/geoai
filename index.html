Improving Data Efficiency of Deep Learning-based Individual Tree Detection and Crown Delineation (ITDCD) in Urban Forests Using Aerial Imagery

University of Canterbury Research Project

Study 1: Semi-supervised ITDCD Study 2: Active Learning for Species ID

Overview

This repository presents research conducted at the University of Canterbury focused on individual tree detection and crown delineation (ITDCD) in urban regions using aerial imagery.

Key Challenges

  • Most existing studies rely on large labelled datasets or cite limited training data as a key constraint. There has been limited analysis of data efficiency and how to achieve high accuracy with fewer samples.
  • The difficulty of annotating tree species has restricted multi-species ITDCD studies, highlighting the need for a more generalisable and cost-efficient framework.
  • Aerial RGB imagery is an accessible data source but underexplored for multi-species urban forest mapping. It remains uncertain whether RGB imagery alone can reliably differentiate multiple species.

Research Goal

The goal of this research is to enhance data efficiency for urban ITDCD using RGB aerial imagery, addressing both general tree detection and multi-species classification.

Research Objectives

  1. General ITDCD: Contrast data efficiency of various deep learning approaches.
    • Compare supervised training under different data sizes.
    • Assess informativeness using entropy measurement.
    • Evaluate copy-paste augmentation and pseudo-label self-training.
  2. Multi-species ITDCD: Develop a data-efficient deep learning approach for species-level crown delineation.
    • Test species classification from individual crown segments.
    • Compare supervised and self-training performance.
    • Apply active learning (entropy-based sample selection).
    • Propose a final integrated framework.

The findings are presented across two studies below:

  • Study 1: Semi-supervised Deep Learning for Urban ITDCD
  • Study 2: Deep Active Learning for Urban Tree Species Identification
